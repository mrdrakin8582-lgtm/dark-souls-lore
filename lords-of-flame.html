Lords of the Flame

“They found the souls of lords within the Flame.”

From the First Flame came great souls. And with them, beings of immense power.

Gwyn, the Lord of Sunlight. The Witch of Izalith and her daughters of chaos.

Nito, the first of the dead. And the Furtive Pygmy, so easily forgotten.

With their newfound power, they challenged the Everlasting Dragons.

Lightning tore through stone scales. Fire engulfed the ancient archtrees.

Thus, the Age of Ancients came to an end.
