Characters

“They who shaped the Age of Fire.”

Lords & Gods

Gwyn

Lord of Sunlight

Bearer of the Light Soul. He led the war against the Everlasting Dragons.

Fearing the coming Dark, he sacrificed himself to link the Flame.

Nito

First of the Dead

The embodiment of death and decay.

His power spread disease and miasma across the world.

Witch of Izalith

Mother of Chaos

A master of fire sorcery.

Her attempt to recreate the First Flame gave birth to Chaos.

Furtive Pygmy

The Lord who was forgotten… yet never truly gone.

One of the Four Great Lords who discovered the Dark Soul.

Unlike the others, the Furtive Pygmy did not seek dominion, but instead divided the Dark Soul, giving birth to humanity.
