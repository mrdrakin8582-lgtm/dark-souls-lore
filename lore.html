The Age of Fire

“But then there was Fire, and with fire came disparity.”

In the Age of Ancients, the world was unformed, shrouded by fog. A land of gray crags, archtrees, and everlasting dragons.

Then there was Fire. And with fire came disparity. Heat and cold, life and death, and of course, light and dark.

From the dark, they came, and found the Souls of Lords within the flame.
