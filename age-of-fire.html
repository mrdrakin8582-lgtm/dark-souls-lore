Age of Fire

“From the Dark, They Came.”

In the Age of Ancients, the world was gray and everlasting. Dragons ruled the land beneath archtrees and stone.

Then, from the Dark, came Fire. And with Fire, disparity was born.

Heat and cold. Life and death. Light and dark.

From the Flame, mighty souls were claimed. Thus began the Age of Fire.
