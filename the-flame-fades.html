The Flame Fades

“The fire began to fade, and darkness crept in.”

The Age of Fire was not meant to last.

As the First Flame weakened, shadows grew longer across the land.

Gwyn, fearing the coming Dark, sought to prolong the Age of Fire.

He sacrificed himself to link the Flame.

Thus, the cycle began.
